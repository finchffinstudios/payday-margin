# Payday Margin Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 11, 2026**

Payday Margin is a personal financial-planning application designed to help users organize bills, plan around paydays, track balances, and understand their financial margin. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the current version of Payday Margin.

## 1. Information You Enter

Payday Margin may allow you to enter information such as:

* Bill names and amounts
* Bill due dates and payment schedules
* Pay schedules and payday information
* Bill-paying balances
* Planned contribution amounts
* Overdue bill information
* Payment-plan information
* Loan or principal balances that you choose to track
* Tasks, notes, and other planning information
* App preferences and settings

This information is provided voluntarily by you for the purpose of using Payday Margin's planning features.

## 2. Local Storage

Payday Margin is designed to store your plan and app state locally on your device.

Payday Margin does not currently require you to create a Payday Margin account, and the app does not currently operate a remote server for storing your financial plan.

Information stored by the app may still be subject to your device manufacturer's, operating system's, or backup provider's backup and security settings. For example, device-level backup services may back up application data depending on your device settings and operating-system configuration.

You should protect access to your device and maintain any backups you consider necessary.

## 3. Information We Collect or Transmit

The current version of Payday Margin is not designed to transmit your entered financial planning information to Payday Margin or to a Payday Margin server.

The current version does not intentionally collect or sell:

* Your bank account credentials
* Credit or debit card numbers
* Social Security numbers
* Your contacts
* Your precise location
* Your photographs
* Your financial institution login information

Payday Margin does not currently connect directly to your bank, employer, lender, utility provider, or other biller.

## 4. Device Authentication

Payday Margin may offer an optional app-lock feature using authentication supported by your device, such as a fingerprint, face recognition, PIN, pattern, or device password.

Authentication is handled through your device's authentication system. Payday Margin does not receive or store a copy of your fingerprint, face scan, device PIN, pattern, or device password.

The app receives only the result necessary to determine whether access was successfully authenticated.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used locally by Payday Margin to provide features such as:

* Organizing upcoming bills
* Planning bills around paydays
* Calculating amounts reserved for bills
* Displaying estimated months of recurring obligations covered
* Determining your displayed Financial State
* Producing planning projections
* Tracking payment progress
* Displaying historical changes within the app
* Remembering your app preferences

## 6. Financial Information

Payday Margin is a personal planning tool.

The financial information displayed by Payday Margin is calculated from information supplied by the user. Payday Margin does not independently verify your account balances, income, bills, due dates, payments, debts, or other financial information.

Payday Margin does not move money, make payments, access bank accounts, or confirm that a payment has been received by a biller.

## 7. Sharing of Information

Payday Margin does not currently sell your personal information.

The current version is not designed to share the financial planning information you enter with advertisers, data brokers, lenders, or other third parties.

Your device or platform provider may separately process information according to its own policies when providing services such as app distribution, device authentication, crash handling, operating-system functions, or device backups. Those services are controlled by their respective providers.

## 8. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of Payday Margin does not contain advertising functionality and is not designed to use your financial planning information for targeted advertising.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your Payday Margin plan is stored locally, your information generally remains on your device until you modify it, delete it through functionality provided by the app, clear the application's stored data, or uninstall the app.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored information.

Payday Margin cannot recover locally stored information that has been permanently deleted unless a separate device-level backup maintained outside Payday Margin is available.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Payday Margin is intended as a personal financial-planning tool and is not directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly design the app to collect personal information from children under 13.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable steps within the design of Payday Margin to help keep app information private, including local storage and optional use of supported device authentication.

However, no device, software application, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and device credentials.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Payday Margin may change over time.

If future versions introduce features such as cloud synchronization, online accounts, analytics, connected financial institutions, advertising, or other services that materially change how information is collected, used, stored, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

The effective date at the top of this policy indicates when the current version became effective.

## 13. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed through the developer contact information shown on Payday Margin's official Google Play listing.
